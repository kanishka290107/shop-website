Our Heritage & Identity

Agra's Definitive Destination for High-End Fashion

Located at the prestigious Shah Market, M.G. Road (near Anjana Cinema), Bacchomal Collection stands as a landmark for discerning shoppers across Agra and Western Uttar Pradesh who appreciate the finer nuances of sartorial craftsmanship.

As "A Collection Shop", our vision was born from a singular passion: to eliminate the distance between global fashion capitals and fashion lovers in Agra. We eliminate the guesswork by personally selecting only the highest-grade, certified genuine garments from internationally celebrated names such as Tommy Hilfiger, Calvin Klein, Superdry, Fred Perry, and Versace.

Whether you are curating a refined corporate wardrobe, hunting for the perfect luxury streetwear fit, or seeking statement designer pieces for special occasions, Bacchomal Collection offers an atmosphere of personalized hospitality, elegance, and exclusivity.

The Principles That Guide Us

The Four Pillars of Bacchomal Collection

Over the years, our customers have trusted us not just as a shop, but as their personal wardrobe curators and fashion advisors.

Uncompromised Authenticity

In a market flooded with counterfeits, Bacchomal Collection is an uncompromising sanctuary of genuine designer wear. Every barcode, stitching line, tag, and fabric is authentic.

Curated Exclusivity

We hand-select each piece to ensure you stand out. We bring exclusive colorways, limited runs, and runway-inspired lines that are otherwise hard to find outside major international metros.

Personalized Service

We believe that true luxury is personal. Our store associates understand cuts, fabric breathability, and body types to ensure every garment you choose fits you impeccably.
